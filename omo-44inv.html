  • Radium: Radium is extremely rare and expensive. Historical data from the early 20th century shows it was valued at $90,000 to $120,000 per gram. Modern estimates suggest it could cost up to $10 million per gram, primarily due to its use in medical treatments.
  • Cocaine: Illicit cocaine is valued at $22,680 per pound, which translates to approximately **$50 per gram**—significantly more than gold by weight. This high price is driven by illegal status and associated risks, not intrinsic production cost.
