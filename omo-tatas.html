4.1.5.1.2. A
General
  • Allegations and investigations linking the Tata Group and the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) primarily center on political financing and corruption cases involving Tata subsidiaries.
Political Donations:
  • The Tata Group routed approximately ₹758 crore to the BJP in April 2024 via the Progressive Electoral Trust, a donation disclosed months after it was made. This occurred shortly after the government approved ₹44,000 crore in subsidies for Tata’s semiconductor plants, leading journalists to investigate potential quid pro quo dynamics, though no illegal intent has been legally proven.
CBI Corruption Case:
  • The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) registered a case in June 2025 against Tata Consulting Engineers (TCE) and former Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust (JNPT) officials. They are accused of a ₹800 crore fraud in a dredging project, involving inflated estimates and criminal conspiracy to favor private bidders.
Historical Allegations:
  • In 2010, Ratan Tata revealed he was asked for a bribe by a minister, highlighting broader corruption issues. In 2016, politician Subramanian Swamy made unproven claims that Ratan Tata was corrupt, citing ethical concerns raised by former chairman Cyrus Mistry.
